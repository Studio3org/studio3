Saved 2: Saved

Opened from the bookmark icon at the top of the Home screen. This is the only place saved pieces live. It is not part of the profile.
Only the person can see their own Saved. Collection on the profile only shows pieces they have actually bought.
Folders show as large picture tiles, 2 across, scrolling up and down. At the top: All saved and Available (both made by the app). Then a small grey label YOUR FOLDERS with a thin line, and below it the folders the person made. The plus at the top right makes a new folder.
Tapping a folder opens it (next screen).
