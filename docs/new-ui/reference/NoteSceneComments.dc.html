Scene viewer: comments

Opens from the comment icon. The sheet covers the bottom part of the screen; the Scene keeps playing behind it, darker.
Each comment: photo, name, time in Geist Mono, text, and Reply. The Scene's artist has a small Artist label. No likes on comments and no reply threads: Reply starts a new comment with @name.
Add a comment field fixed at the bottom. Press and hold a comment to report it or delete your own. The artist can delete any comment on their Scene and turn comments off for a Scene from the more menu.
