Drafts

Opened from Go to drafts (after a failed publish), from Drafts on the What are you posting sheet (after tapping +).
Every piece or Scene they started but did not post is saved here automatically, newest first. Filter: All, Pieces, Scenes.
Each row: picture (3:4 for pieces, 9:16 for Scenes), title (or Untitled piece), what is left in plain words, and the type and last edited time in Geist Mono.
A draft that failed to publish shows the ! circle and the line in red (#B3261E) with Didn't publish. Tap to try again. Tapping any draft reopens posting at the step they stopped on.
Press and hold, or swipe left, to delete a draft (asks to confirm). Drafts never expire. Empty state: the light grey logo mark with No drafts.
