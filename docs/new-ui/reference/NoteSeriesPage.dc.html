Series page

Opened from a series row on Home, a series on a profile, or the Part of a series card on a piece page.
Top: SERIES · number of pieces (Geist Mono), the series name, the artist with Follow, then the artist's description of the series (only if they wrote one).
All pieces ordered by the date they were posted, newest first, 2 across, 3:4, each with its status: Available and price (green dot), Collected (iridescent label), Not for sale, or the current bid and time left. Collected pieces always stay in the series.
Available filters to pieces people can buy. Behind this series: Scenes linked to any piece in the series, opening the Scene viewer.
When the artist views their own series, an Edit series button replaces Follow.
Not for sale pieces show no chip; only Available and Collected get chips.
