How this canvas is laid out

Rows are flows. Read each row left to right in the order a person moves through it.
The first card in every row explains the flow: where it starts, what it connects to, and the rules that matter.
Design rules (colors, type, spacing, components) are on the next board in this column. Use them over anything that looks different in a screen.
Not designed yet is the third board: missing screens, open decisions, and what is planned for later. Sizes and scaling is the fourth: the phone template, every card size, and how layouts adapt.
Boards with a blue mark are interactive. Press Play to scroll or swipe them.
Component specs live in Airtable: Studio 3, Design System, Tasks. Each task has the full spec and rules.
Screen-by-screen explanations are in the Dev Handoff Spec: purpose, data, behavior and edge cases for every board here.

The flows

1. Sign in and accountThe first screen after download, the signed-out profile, and the email code sign-in. Triggered by any action like save, follow, collect or post.
2. BrowseHome feed, Explore, Behind the work, event page, event card variant.
3. ProfilesA seller, a collector, and your own profile.
4. Post a pieceCover, availability, details, materials, review, published, error, and listing a piece later.
5. Post a ScenePhoto first, then video. Edit screens are dark.
6. Piece page and collectingPiece page for fixed price, auction and not for sale, messaging the seller, checkout, collect confirmation.
7. BiddingBid sheet, leading and outbid, won, checkout, collected, payment failed, second chance, lost, closed.
