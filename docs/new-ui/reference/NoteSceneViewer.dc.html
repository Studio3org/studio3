Scene viewer: video

Opens full screen when someone taps a Scene (Behind the work, a piece page, a profile, Explore). Swipe up for the next Scene, down for the previous, in the same order as where they came from.
9:16 videos fill the screen and play right away, muted until they tap the sound icon (sound stays on for the rest of the session). Tap to pause. A thin white line at the bottom shows progress. Videos loop.
Bottom left: artist photo, name and Follow; caption (2 lines, tap to see all); then the linked piece as one plain line: a small 3:4 thumbnail, the title in white, the price in softer white and an arrow. No box behind it. Tapping it opens the piece page.
Right side: Save and Comments (each with its count in Geist Mono) and Share. No likes. Top: back, sound, and more (report, copy link).
