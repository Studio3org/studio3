Notifications: in-app outbid alert

When someone is already using the app, a lock screen notification does not appear. For Outbid only, show this small card that slides down from the top of whatever screen they are on.
It shows the piece picture, the red word Outbid, the message, and a Bid button with the next minimum amount. Tapping the button opens the bid screen. Tapping anywhere else on the card opens the piece page.
It slides away by itself after 6 seconds, or when swiped up. If they are already on that piece page, do not show it; the bid bar updates instead.
