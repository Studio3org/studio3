Flow 2

Browse

Starts from: Opening the app. Home and Explore are tabs in the bottom nav.

Home is one piece at a time, edge to edge, artist row below the art. Series rows and event cards appear between pieces.
Series rows swipe between pieces. Feed pieces swipe between photos of one piece.
Event cards only for events in the next two weeks, once per feed load, never next to a series row.
Explore is shelves (Available now, Behind the work, New series, Upcoming shows, Artists to know), then a masonry grid ordered by momentum.
See all on any shelf opens its full list. Upcoming shows opens the event page.
Saved opens from the bookmark on Home. Folders, plus Available filled in by the app. Private, separate from Collection.
