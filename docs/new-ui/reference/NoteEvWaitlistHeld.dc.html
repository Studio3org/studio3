Events 6c: tickets held for you

Opened from the Waitlist ticket open notification.
Their held tickets are already picked. The bar at the top counts down the time left in red Geist Mono (2 hours, 30 minutes or 10 minutes, depending on how close the event is).
Other ticket types still show Sold out. Continue to payment goes to the normal checkout. The countdown at the top is the only time reminder on this screen. When it hits zero, the screen closes and the tickets go to the next person.
If they also waited for another ticket type, a Keep waiting for [type] link shows under the button. Tapping it gives up these held tickets (they go to the next person) and keeps their place in line for the other type only.
