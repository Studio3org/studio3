Piece page: collected (your piece)

What the collector sees on a piece they bought. The artist row keeps Follow, shown as Following (light outline with a small check) when they already follow the artist. No Save.
Shipping is not part of the piece details. While the piece is on its way, the bottom bar becomes a large delivery card (white, thin iridescent border) in place of the Collected button: Arrives Nov 13 to 16 and an On time label (green, or Delayed in red); a tracker of four small round icons (22px) joined by thin bars (collected, packed, shipped, delivered; finished steps show a check, the current step is black, its bar partly filled); then the current step (On its way) with one line of detail and an arrow that opens carrier tracking. Under the card: Message Amara as a grey link. Once delivered, the bar goes back to the Collected button with message.
The more menu (top right) has Hide from my Collection, which removes it from their public Collection tab without affecting anything else.
