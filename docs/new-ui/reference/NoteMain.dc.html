Dev notes

Home feed

Default tab. One piece at a time, personalized from the intention answer, then by activity.
Standalone piece: full-bleed, square corners, height follows the art with a cap. Artist row below: 32px avatar, name, title, status chip.
Swiping a feed image changes photos of that piece. Dots only with 2+ photos, bottom center.
Series row: cards about 80% width, radius 12, next card peeks, snap scroll. Cover only, no internal swipe. Last card is See all X pieces.
Event card: only events in the next 2 weeks, once per feed load, never next to a series row.
Spacing: 20px between pieces, 48px around inset sections. Top bar: All filter, wordmark logo file, Saved, inbox (a small red dot when anything is unread in activity or messages).
Event cards in the home feed keep the EVENT tag so they stand out among the art. Everywhere else (Events tab, event page) shows the category instead, like GALLERY WALK. Tapping an event card opens the event page in Flow 9.
Feed layout: pieces are inset with the 16-point screen margins (still 3:4, never cropped). Under each piece, a label 12 points below it: artist name (medium weight, opens their profile) and title with the year in grey on the left; on the right, in small grey Geist Mono, the medium and, under it, the status chip: Available (green dot) or Collected (iridescent pill). Nothing for Not for sale. No photo, no price.
52 points between posts (series and event cards too), so the space separates posts. No lines or cards.
