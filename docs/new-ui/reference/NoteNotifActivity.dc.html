Notifications 2: Activity

The inbox icon in the home top bar opens this. A small red dot on it means something is unread in Activity or Messages; no number. No settings icon here; notification settings live in profile settings. Tabs Activity and Messages; Messages shows an unread count.
Grouped Today and This week. Unread rows are medium weight (500) in Ink, read rows regular grey. Names and titles inside a row are medium, never semibold. Times in Geist Mono.
Status words use the status colors: Outbid and Ending soon (under 24 hours) in red, Now listed in green.
Bundled: saves and follows group into one row per piece per day. Tapping a row opens the piece, profile, event or order.
