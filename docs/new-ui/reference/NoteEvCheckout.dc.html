Events 2: checkout

Same layout as buying a piece: event summary at the top, then rows for Tickets (tap to change), Sent to (their email), and Payment (card through Stripe).
Order summary lists each ticket type with its count (Free if free), then Sales tax and Total. No service fee for buyers.
Orders with only free tickets skip this screen and go straight to the confirmation after Reserve.
Every ticket price shown before checkout is the full price. The total here is those prices plus sales tax, nothing else.
Studio 3 never charges buyers a fee. Our fees come out of the artist's or host's payout. The price shown is the full price; only sales tax is added at checkout (plus shipping and optional insurance for art that ships).
