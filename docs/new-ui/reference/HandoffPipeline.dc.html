Decided since the last update

Notifications: done. Inbox (Activity and Messages), conversation, new message, settings, the ask sheet, lock screen and the full notification catalog are in Flow 8.
Saved: done. Opens from the bookmark on Home, private, with folders and the Available folder. Collection only shows owned pieces.
Bidding: fixed end time, no extensions, no maximum bids for now.
Increments: $25 under $500, $50 to $999, $100 to $4,999, $250 to $9,999, $500 above. Minimum next bid is the current bid plus the increment.
Cards: verified on the first bid, no hold. The winner confirms shipping and insurance, then pays within 48 hours.
Reserve: shows Reserve not met yet, never the amount.
Status colors: green for top bidder, red for outbid and time under 24 hours. Bidding only.
Not for sale and list later: owner view, the details sheet, pricing and listed screens are done.
Collection: View your collection opens the buyer's Collection with the new piece first.
Events: tags say EVENT or UPCOMING SHOW, with the venue in the details line.

Counsel questions

Confirm with counsel the exact wording and record-keeping for the Terms and Privacy Policy agreement (EU rules), and that requiring an account before browsing is fine for App Store review.
Whether Texas auctioneer licensing applies to timed online auctions with no auctioneer.
What terms must say about reserve auctions and seller withdrawal, and whether bidders may retract bids.
Whether Reserve not met yet, without the amount, is enough disclosure.
Whether winning bids are binding and second chance offers are allowed.
Whether to require 18+ for bidding and buying, given the 16+ app rating.
Whether auction texts can use sign-in consent or need their own opt-in.
Sales tax on sales, shipping and insurance, and other states' rules for out-of-state bidders.
