Events 4: your events

Opened from the ticket icon at the top right of the Events tab. One page that scrolls, no tabs.
Upcoming: events they have tickets for, soonest first. The next one is a large card (flyer, how soon it is, name, date and time, venue and ticket count, and a Show tickets button). Tapping it opens the QR code.
Any other upcoming events follow as smaller rows under the large card, in the same Upcoming section.
Saved: events they bookmarked but have not bought tickets for, as a sideways row of flyers with the date and Only 3 left (red) or Sold out (grey). Tapping opens the event page. Buying a ticket moves it up; it disappears after the event ends.
Past events: one row at the bottom that opens a simple list of past events.
No tickets: start with Saved. Nothing at all: show the empty state with the light grey logo mark.
