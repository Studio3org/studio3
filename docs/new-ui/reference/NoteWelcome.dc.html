First launch: welcome

First screen after download. Intro animation, about 2 seconds, plays once per install.
Sequence: the three logo circles pop in largest to smallest (0.15s, 0.35s, 0.5s), the wordmark fades up (0.75s), artwork tiles drift in from the edges (1.0 to 1.35s), then the line and buttons fade up (1.6 to 1.95s).
Tiles are real pieces from the catalog, rotated per launch, and keep a slow 5s drift after the intro. Reduced motion: show the final frame with no movement.
