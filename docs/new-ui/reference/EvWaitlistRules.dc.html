Waitlist rules

How the event waitlist works, step by step. Use these exact numbers.

Joining

On a sold-out event, Join waitlist opens a sheet that works like Choose tickets: a card for each ticket type with − / + buttons. They can wait for more than one type at once, up to the host's per-order limit for each.
No card is needed to join and it is free.
They can leave the waitlist at any time (tap You're on the waitlist on the event page).

Who gets tickets first

First come, first served, in the order people joined.
When tickets open up (someone gets a refund or the host adds more), they are held for the next person in line who asked for that ticket type (up to the number they asked for).
If more tickets open up than that person wants, the rest go to the next people in line at the same time.

How long the hold lasts

2 hours to buy, normally.
30 minutes when the event is less than 24 hours away.
10 minutes when the event is less than 2 hours away.

What they see

A lock screen notification, an email, and a text if they opted in: 2 tickets are held for you until 9:40 PM CST.
Opening it shows Choose tickets with their tickets already picked and a countdown in red Geist Mono at the top.

Waiting for more than one type

Whichever of their ticket types opens first is held for them. On the held screen they can buy it, or tap Keep waiting for [other type] to give it up and keep their place in line for the other type only.

If they do not buy in time

The hold ends, the tickets go to the next person in line, and they are taken off the waitlist. They get a notification saying so.

Other cases

If the host adds more tickets than the whole waitlist wants, the waitlist holds go out first and the rest go on sale to everyone right away.
Free events work the same way, with Reserve instead of paying.
The waitlist closes when the event starts. Anyone still waiting is removed quietly.
The host can see how many people are waiting and for which ticket types.
