Artist: your auction, live

The artist's own piece page while their auction runs. YOUR AUCTION: current bid large, time left (Geist Mono, red under 24 hours), then number of bids, reserve status and starting bid.
Reserve status is only shown to the artist: Reserve met (green) or Below reserve (grey). Buyers never see the reserve amount.
Bids: newest first. Everyone, including the artist, sees bidders as Bidder 1, Bidder 2 (the same person keeps the same number in one auction). The artist sees the winner's name only after payment. Press and hold a bid to report it.
Nothing about the auction can be edited once the first bid is in. Before the first bid, Edit and Cancel are in the more menu instead.
Bottom: Share auction (main) and End auction early (grey link).
