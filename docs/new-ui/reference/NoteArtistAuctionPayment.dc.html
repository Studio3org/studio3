Artist: winner's payment failed

When an auction ends, the winner's saved card is charged automatically. If it works, the page becomes the collected piece page for artists (delivery card, payout).
If the charge fails, the winner gets 12 hours to update their card. The artist sees this card: the red ! and Payment didn't go through, the deadline, and what happens next.
After 12 hours without payment, the piece is offered to the next highest bidder at their highest bid (second chance, same as the bidding flow). The artist is told either way.
