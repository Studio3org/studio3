Scene viewer: photo, not 9:16

Photos and videos that are not 9:16 (3:4, square, landscape) sit in the middle at their real shape, with a blurred, darker copy of the same image filling the space around them. No black bars.
A Scene can have several photos: swipe left and right between them (the dots show which). Swiping up still goes to the next Scene.
With more than one linked piece, the small thumbnails overlap and the line says 2 linked pieces. Tapping it opens the pieces sheet.
