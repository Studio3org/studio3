Dev notes

Settings: privacy

Private account: when on, only approved followers see their profile, pieces, Scenes and Collection; new followers send a request (Follow requests). Seller profiles cannot be private, since listings must be public; turning on selling asks them to go public.
Who can message you: Everyone, People you follow, or No one.
Private Collection: hides the Collection tab from everyone but them, even followers. Off by default.
See your profile as others do: opens their profile as a stranger would see it.
Hidden pieces: pieces they hid one by one from their public Collection; tap to unhide.
Blocked accounts: blocked people cannot message them, see their profile or bid on their auctions. Tap to unblock.
