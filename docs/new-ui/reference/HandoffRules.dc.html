Design rules

The system behind every screen. If a mock and this board disagree, this board wins.

Color

Gallery White
#FAFAF8
App background. Nearly neutral so art reads true.
Paper
#F0F0EE
Secondary surfaces: search bar, insights card, summary boxes.
Line
#E3E3E0
Dividers and light outlines. #D4D4D0 for button outlines.
Graphite
#6B6B6B
Secondary text. #8E8E8E for tertiary text and inactive icons.
Ink
#111111
Text, primary buttons, nav, event cards, dark edit screens.
Iridescent
gradient, slow shift
Crisp and shifting on the Collected chip and the collect confirmation. A soft, blurred version is the sign-in background. A thin foil strip or border on event tickets (You're going and the ticket), the frame on Your event is live, and the frame around the preview on every Published or Listed screen (pieces and Scenes). Nowhere else.

No accent color. No red outside bidding status: errors use Ink text plus an icon. The old palette (Kiln, Darkroom Amber, Raw Linen, Photo Blue, Studio Sienna) is retired.

Type

GeistAll UI. Weights stay light: medium (500) is the heaviest used, for titles, names, buttons and anything selected; everything else is regular (400). Titles 24 to 32, headers 17 to 20, body 14 to 16, labels 13, captions 12. Prices are Geist.
Geist MonoFacts only: dates, times, counts, dimensions, chips, tags, code digits, record numbers. Never sentences, names, prices or shipping times.
studio 3Outfit is brand only: the wordmark and marketing. In the app, the wordmark ships as a logo file.

Component rules

SelectionOption rows: selected is bold Ink with a thin vertical line on the left. Others fade to grey. No radio buttons, no checkmarks, no boxes.
FieldsLabel above, value on a single underline. Focused underline is Ink.
TooltipsInfo icon next to a label. Tap to open a dark box under the field, tap anywhere to close, one open at a time. Anything everyone needs is shown inline, never only in a tooltip.
Bottom barsInfo row on top (label above value, both sides), full-width button below, outlined message button beside it. Fades in from the page, no border line.
Dark screensWhere someone adjusts images (photo edit, video trim, cover) and the full-screen Scene viewer. Everything else is light. Crossfade in and out.
Top of imagesLabels and icons on images (Cover, 1 / 4, photo counts, video length, play icons, Just collected) are plain white text with only a faint text shadow for legibility. No background, circles or pills. Top icons on piece pages sit over a soft gradient. Status chips like Available and Collected stay pills.
ArtPieces are 3:4 in grids and shelves and never cropped or edited. Scenes can be edited and snap to 3:4, 1:1 or landscape in grids.
Status wordsCollect for the action, Collected for sold, never Sold. Collection is what someone owns.
Status colorsOnly two status chips exist: Available (green dot) and Collected (iridescent). Not for sale is plain text, never a chip. Errors are red too (see Errors). The only exceptions to monochrome. Available chips use a green dot #2E8B57. Green #1E6B3C on #E7F2EA for You're the top bidder. Red #B3261E on #FBEAE8 for You've been outbid, red for time remaining under 24 hours, a small red dot after the label of any required field that is still empty, and a red dot on the inbox icon when something is unread. Always paired with an icon or words, never color alone.
Follow buttonFollow: Ink fill, white text (on dark images: white fill, Ink text). Following: light outline, Ink text, with a small check before the word. Same size, so tapping just swaps the look.
MotionShort and quiet, about 0.2 to 0.3s. Everything respects reduced motion.
