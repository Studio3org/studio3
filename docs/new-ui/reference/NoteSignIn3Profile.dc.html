Sign in 3: profile setup (new only)

New accounts only. Optional photo, name, username (regular Geist) with live availability check, optional phone for shipping and delivery updates.
Before Continue: an unticked box, I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (both links open the full text). Continue stays grey until it is ticked. This is required by EU rules and must be its own action (nothing else is bundled with it). Save the date and time they agreed and which version of each document. Returning users who already agreed never see it again; if the documents change, ask again once.
Password is required: at least 8 characters, with an eye to show or hide it. Continue stays grey until there is a valid password and the Terms box is ticked. They can change it later in settings.
No marketing texts opt-in here. Text preferences live in notification settings.
