Notification catalog

Every notification, what triggers it, the exact wording, and where tapping it goes. Bracketed text and amounts are examples.

How to read this table

Each row is one notification the app sends. Read the row from left to right, like a sentence: when this happens, this person gets a notification. On their phone's lock screen it shows these two lines. Inside the app it looks like this. When they tap it, this screen opens.

The grey labels in capitals (COLLECTING, FOLLOWING, YOUR WORK, EVERYONE) group the rows by who they are for. Text in [brackets] and the amounts are examples; use the real piece name, price and time.

Example: the first row

When someone places a higher bid than Jordan, Jordan gets a notification. His lock screen shows Outbid on Bodies in Color on the first line and Someone bid $3,700. Bid $3,800 to take it back. on the second. In the app's Inbox it shows the piece with the red word Outbid. Tapping it opens the piece page with the bid screen already open. It is sent every time this happens. Jordan can turn it off with the Auctions switch. In the last 24 hours of the auction it is also sent as a text message, if Jordan added a phone number and agreed to texts.

Writing rules. Title says what happened, body says what to do or what it means. Name the piece or person, never "your item." No em dashes, no exclamation marks except Delivered. Amounts as $3,700.
Status colors. Only Outbid and Ending soon (red, under 24h) and Now listed (green) carry a status word. Everything else is plain.
Texts. Only where marked, only for people who added a phone and opted in. Email is always on for receipts, payouts and payment issues.
