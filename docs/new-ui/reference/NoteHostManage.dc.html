Hosting 7: manage event

The event cover at full width, crisp and 3:4 (never blurred), with how soon it is as a small white label in the corner (In 3 days, Tomorrow, Tonight). Name, then date, time and venue in Geist Mono.
Four numbers side by side, like the stats on a profile: tickets sold (of the total), earned so far (after the Studio 3 fee), waitlist, saved. Numbers in Geist Mono, small grey labels under them.
Ticket types as simple rows with how many are sold. A full type says Sold out in red. No bars or charts.
Check in guests sits in a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen (like the bar on piece and event pages), so it is always one tap away. Above the button: Checked in (0 of 62, updating live) and Doors open time.
Rows without icons: Guest list, Edit event, Reschedule. Cancel event is a grey text link at the bottom so it is not tapped by accident. Share is the icon at the top right.
