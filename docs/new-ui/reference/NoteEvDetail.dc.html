Events 0b: event page

Opened by tapping any event. The flyer fills the top with back, save, share and more as white icons over a soft dark shadow. Category, name, venue and date sit on the bottom of the flyer.
Hosted by with a Follow button. Then Details (cut after 4 lines with Show more), Featured artists (tap to open their profile), Pieces at this event (tap to open the piece), Location (address and a map that opens directions), and FAQs written by the host that open and close.
The bar at the bottom always stays visible: From (lowest ticket price, or Free) and the date, then Get tickets, which opens Choose tickets.
The bottom bar shows the ticket price range (Free to $25). Prices are full prices; only tax is added at checkout.
Pieces at this event show how they are delivered (Pickup at the event or Ships after the event), as set by the host.
Every event time in the app shows the time zone (for example 8:00 PM CST), taken from the event location. Guests in other time zones still see the event's local time.
